## Scope and limitations

This demonstrates synthetic-data ingestion, native Splunk search validation, negative controls and scheduled alert firing in a Splunk Cloud trial. It does not demonstrate production monitoring, Splunk Enterprise Security deployment, live DNS collection, confirmed tunneling or exfiltration. The historical all-time replay is a lab test, not a production scheduling design. Fixed buckets can miss boundary-straddling bursts. Splunk dedup retains events by search order; this fixture's duplicate has identical content, so these results do not establish equivalence to Python's first-input-occurrence policy for conflicting duplicate IDs.
